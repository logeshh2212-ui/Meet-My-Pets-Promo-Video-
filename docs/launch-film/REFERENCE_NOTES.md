# Reference: "Best SaaS Product Launch Ad Video | LangEase"

Link: https://youtu.be/SgmuplXU2iY. A 33 s, 16:9 ad for a desktop SaaS product, made by the studio "zelios". I stepped through it about every 0.7 s, with closer looks at the transitions.

We copy its **techniques only**: not its blue palette, its desktop UI or its branding.

## What happens, second by second
| Time | What we see | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | "Turn Books" → "Audio" → "Any" | **Kinetic type**: 1–2 words at a time, centred on a near-white background. Each word blurs and scales in on a beat. The key word is in a gradient colour, the others are black |
| 3.0–4.6 | "Any language [glass folder icon] Instantly" | A **glassy 3D icon slides in between the words** and pushes them apart. The words fade, the icon centres, and a **cursor hand taps it** |
| 4.6–5.7 | The icon drops into a frosted-glass window | **Morph:** the window grows *around* the icon; the icon dissolves into it (drag-and-drop shown as motion) |
| 6.0–6.7 | "Just drop and go" | Word-by-word build, alternating colours |
| 6.7–10.0 | Four phones form the walls of a **tunnel**; "Books. Audio." → "All In One Platform" in the centre | **Phone tunnel:** the camera dollies forward through the screens while the text builds letter by letter with blur |
| 10.0–10.9 | One phone whips past and leaves a **blue motion streak** | **Match cut:** the streak becomes a horizontal bar |
| 11.0–13.0 | The bar fills, with a counter 86/100 → 98 → 100 | **Progress → result:** the full bar shrinks into a pill button |
| 13.0–14.4 | Button → glass circle with a ✓, "Done", confetti | **Morph + one celebration burst**, then a quick fade |
| 14.4–16.5 | A row of cards slides in; pull back to the **whole app UI, tilted in 3D** | Detail first, then context; the camera glides over a perspective UI |
| 17.2–17.9 | The cursor taps a card; **the card lifts out of the UI and fills the frame** | **Container lift** |
| 18.6–20.0 | The card **multiplies** into 4 tilted copies scattered in depth around "Multiple Languages" | **Depth scatter:** near cards blurred, far cards sharp; slow drift |
| 20.7–22.1 | Back to the tilted UI (list view); tap; **fast whip up** with motion blur | Whip transition |
| 22.8–24.2 | Macro on one list row → slides to the "Distribute To Youtube" button; tap; the button isolates and turns gradient | **Macro → isolate:** one control becomes the hero |
| 24.9–26.3 | The button **morphs into a 4-point sparkle star**, which flies off trailing a ✓ path | The brand glyph is born from the UI |
| 26.3–28.4 | "Translate. Dub. Distribute" builds; the sparkle rides at the end of the line like a cursor | Three-word tagline on beats |
| 28.4–29.1 | The text slides out; **the sparkle stays and becomes part of the logo** (J + sparkle) | **The logo is assembled from an element seen earlier** |
| 29.1–33.0 | "LangEase" wordmark; the URL fades in below; ~4 s hold | Clean end card on a plain light background |

## What makes it feel premium
1. **Everything morphs into the next thing:** icon → window, streak → bar → button → ✓, button → sparkle → logo. There are almost no plain cuts.
2. **One idea per moment.** Only 1–3 words on screen at once, and something changes about every 0.7 s.
3. **A clean, bright, almost empty background.** Depth comes from soft shadows, glass and blur, not from busy backdrops.
4. **Glass material** (frosted, translucent, soft inner light) on icons and buttons.
5. **A cursor/hand icon** shows every interaction, so the viewer always knows what was tapped.
6. **The brand glyph appears inside the product before it becomes the logo**, so the ending feels earned.
7. **Restraint:** one colour family, one confetti burst in the whole film, and a long calm hold on the logo.

## What we took: principles only (applied in storyboard v2)
We did **not** reuse any of its gags: no phone tunnel, no progress-bar counter, no ✓ + confetti, no cursor hand, no card-multiply scatter. We took the ideas underneath and rebuilt them from MeetMyPets' own world:

| Principle from the reference | Our own version |
|---|---|
| Things morph instead of cutting | **The Two Loops**: Shiro's coral line and Tom's amber line carry every transition and finally *become* the logo (see 02_storyboard.md, "Visual language") |
| The brand glyph appears before the logo | The double-loop is the Discover tab (S13), the match symbol (S15), and is woven from the two pets' paths at the end (S21) |
| One idea per beat, short type | **Tag type**: 1–3 words per beat; the key word hangs in a collar-tag pill |
| Interactions are always visible | **Paw-tap**: a paw-pad press from the app's Paw icon |
| Cuts have meaning | **Swipe cuts**: the app's own swipe-right / scroll-up gestures change the act (3 times only) |
| Progress feels rewarding | **Ring → loop**: the real completion ring closes and stretches into Shiro's loop (no confetti) |
| Clean, calm ending | The woven logo on a clean warm background, then a still hold |

## What we don't copy
- The blue / indigo palette (ours: coral, orange, amber)
- The desktop SaaS UI
- The "zelios" watermark
- Their exact layouts and timings
